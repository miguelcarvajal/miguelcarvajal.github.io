Miguel Carvajal, PhD, is Full Professor of Journalism at Miguel Hernández University (UMH) in Elche, Spain. His research focuses on the transformation of the news industry, particularly journalism innovation and business models for digital media. He has authored or co-authored more than 65 scholarly publications, including 28 articles in indexed journals such as Digital Journalism, Journalism Studies, Journalism Practice and El Profesional de la Información, and book chapters with Routledge, Springer and Edward Elgar.

He has directed the Master's Programme in Journalism Innovation since 2013 and the Communication Research Group (GICOV) since 2022. He is principal investigator of Novacult (PID2022-138194OB-I00), a project on organizational culture and innovation in journalism funded by the Spanish Ministry of Science and Innovation. He has taken part in 16 competitive national and European research projects, including IBERIFIER (European Commission) and JoIn-DemoS (German Research Foundation). He was a visiting scholar at Fordham University's McGannon Center (New York, 2010) with a José Castillejo fellowship, and at the University of Westminster (London, 2004).
